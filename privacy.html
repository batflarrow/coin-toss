Privacy Policy

Coin Toss for Apple Watch · Last updated 17 August 2026

Coin Toss collects nothing. It has no analytics, no accounts, no advertising, and no network connection of any kind. Nothing you do in the app leaves your watch.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. There is no signup, no login, and no tracking of any kind — including no advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device

The app saves two preferences locally so it can remember them between launches:

  • Which coin design you selected
  • Whether toss sounds are switched on

These are stored in the app's own storage on your device (UserDefaults). They are never transmitted anywhere, and they are removed when you delete the app. Your toss history and running tally exist only while the app is open and are not written to disk at all.

Network access

The app makes no network requests while running. Every coin image and sound is bundled inside the app itself, so it works fully offline and there is no server for it to contact.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising frameworks, or crash reporters. No third party receives any data from it.

The coin photographs and sound effects bundled with the app come from public-domain and CC0 sources, each credited in CREDITS.md. Using them involves no data exchange at runtime.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.
